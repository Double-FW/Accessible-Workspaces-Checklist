# Terminology alignment

The inspected [Double-FW Terminology and Principles](https://github.com/Double-FW/Terminology-and-Principles) revision is `219b5feddd271bcdf70ea0ac0c4bc5c62fe9a0f4`. Its [governance](https://github.com/Double-FW/Terminology-and-Principles/blob/219b5feddd271bcdf70ea0ac0c4bc5c62fe9a0f4/GOVERNANCE.md) and [decision register](https://github.com/Double-FW/Terminology-and-Principles/blob/219b5feddd271bcdf70ea0ac0c4bc5c62fe9a0f4/review/DECISIONS.md) distinguish scoped approved resolutions from proposed individual definitions. This alignment does not approve every definition or adopt an employer’s policy.

| Distinction | Workspace application |
| --- | --- |
| Accessibility and inclusion | Investigate real barriers to necessary work, welfare, support and participation; a route, room or device existing is not enough. |
| Task completion and task time | Preserve correct outcomes, quality, effort, chosen assistance and independence. A fast or assisted route does not establish equivalent access. |
| Content consumption and engagement | Examine access to instructions, maps and meaningful participation; more time may indicate difficulty. |
| Choice and preferences | Explore relevant routes, settings and support without inferring diagnosis or assuming one quiet space, light level or tool suits a group. |
| Equivalent/comparable experience (D02) | Compare outcomes, effort, privacy, availability, control and dignity rather than identical routes or equipment. |
| Satisfactory and satisfaction (D01) | Satisfactory is the proposed experience principle; satisfaction is a person’s assessment. A positive rating cannot waive a barrier or applicable requirement. |
| Emotional lenses (D03) | Feeling Seen, Feeling Capable, Feeling Confident, Feeling Safe and Feeling Valued are intended qualities/research lenses, not emotions inferred from a pass. Feeling Safe does not prove physical safety, security or fire compliance. |

The checklist’s P/N/C labels are broad source-owned scanning tags, not diagnoses, mutually exclusive demographic groups, Cognitive UX identifiers or complete EAA functional profiles. Keep AW-01–AW-21 in this checklist’s namespace. Use named Cognitive UX `CP-01`–`CP-10` references where relevant (D04). W3C ACT, local ACT-style procedures and user-oriented task scripts have different source/status meanings (D05).

WCAG robustness, Agentic Robustness and a particular route/system’s reliability have different scopes (D06). Digital accessibility ratios or automated tools do not establish the usability or safety of a physical space. Preserve observations, participant reports, measurements, supplier claims, estimates, hypotheses, synthetic examples, proposals and approvals (D07). Use the [record guide](Workspace_Review_and_Handover.md#record-progress-and-evidence-separately) for the difference between review progress, check results and actual decisions; unknown, not tested and not applicable differ.

Use adopted Double-FW meanings for its own methods and preserve externally owned legal/technical meanings and actual applicability (D08). A local design recommendation, an adopted project requirement and a statutory duty are different. A completed checklist, validated JSON, matching metadata or repository merge is not a physical inspection, acceptance or policy approval.

Respect identity and language preferences, including Deaf and neurodivergent identities. Do not infer needs, clinical status, emotion, consent, capability or strengths from group membership or behaviour. Source, editorial, inspection, participant-feedback and human-decision dates are separate events.
