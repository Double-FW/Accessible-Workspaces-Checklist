# Related public Double-FW guidance

These repositories were confirmed public on 22 November 2026. They are optional specialist routes, not private dependencies or copied source bodies. Record the actual revision and sections used. A link does not establish source inspection, policy adoption, legal applicability or rights to reproduce the content.

| Resource | Relevant use |
| --- | --- |
| [Terminology and Principles](https://github.com/Double-FW/Terminology-and-Principles) | Shared meanings, source authority, evidence and status. |
| [Disabled employee lifecycle](https://github.com/Double-FW/Disabled-Employee-Lifecycle) | Connect workspace barriers to attraction, onboarding, work, progression and exit without expanding this checklist’s scope. |
| [Inclusion for managers and HR systems](https://github.com/Double-FW/Inclusion-for-Managers-and-HR-Systems) | Support conversations, responsibilities, transition continuity, meeting/communication prompts and confidential blank structures. |
| [Accessibility programme runbooks](https://github.com/Double-FW/Accessibility_Programme_Runbooks) | Plan local delivery, owners, dependencies and improvement; no operational assignment is established by a link. |
| [Inclusion and accessibility policy](https://github.com/Double-FW/Inclusion-and-Accessibility-Policy) | Separate commitments, actual policy adoption, evidence and legal-applicability questions. |
| [QA accessibility testing](https://github.com/Double-FW/QA_Accessibility_Testing) | Relevant digital interface evidence for booking, entry and workplace systems; not a physical access or fire-safety assessment. |
| [Cognitive UX design](https://github.com/Double-FW/Cognitive-UX-Design) | Clarity, navigation, instructions, memory demands and choice, with individual context and owning identifiers. |
| [Data ethics and disability](https://github.com/Double-FW/Data_Ethics_and_Disability) | Participation, minimisation, privacy and responsible use of group information. |
| [Accessible digital and printed documents](https://github.com/Double-FW/Accessible_Digital_and_Printed_Documents) | Pre-arrival guides, signs/instructions, review materials and appropriate alternative reading formats. |

Use the local [review and handover guide](Workspace_Review_and_Handover.md) for responsibilities, meeting setups and verification without needing an external governance/RACI or events repository. If a public route later becomes unavailable, state the missing evidence, continue independent authorised work from local guidance and do not invent the linked contents. For dimensions, specialist equipment, building/safety/fire decisions and current obligations, use appropriate primary sources and competent expertise; these repositories do not replace them.
